# Moodrobe usability protocol
Version: moodrobe-usability-2026-10-21-v1 · 21 October 2026

## Purpose
Observe whether adults can understand and use the prototype. This is a formative usability test, not evidence of emotional, medical or sustainability benefits.

## Recruitment and consent
Invite 5–8 consenting adults aged 18 or older. Participation is voluntary; no names or contact details belong in exported files. Give each person a distinct code (P01, P02…). Explain the browser-only recording, optional export and right to stop before starting. Do not recruit minors under this protocol. Obtain any institutional approval required for a separate formal study.

## Procedure (10–15 minutes)
1. Open the guided sample demo and select “Take part in a usability session”. Read and accept the consent statements yourself; the facilitator must not accept for you.
2. Set your mood and occasion; save the check-in.
3. Generate a real neural edit. Record first-run waiting time separately in facilitator notes.
4. Inspect an outfit and save it.
5. Record an outfit in the wear diary.
6. Find the model explanation and limitations in the Lab.
7. Finish the session, rate ease and whether you would wear a suggestion, and optionally describe confusion. Unfinished tasks remain incomplete.
8. Download the JSON only if you choose to share it. No automatic upload occurs. Do not include sensitive information in comments.

Task timings are cumulative milliseconds since consent, not isolated task durations. Sample wardrobes and personal/mixed wardrobes must be reported separately. Do not use camera, microphone or biometric tools for this test. The research dashboard imports files locally and deduplicates session IDs; session counts do not establish unique participants. Check anonymous codes manually.

## Analysis and reporting
Open /research.html and import the files participants elected to share. Report sample size, recruitment, date, wardrobe condition, task completion, descriptive ease ratings, obstacles and limitations. The dashboard intentionally starts empty. Retain an observation-to-change log: date | anonymous code | observed difficulty | decision | verification. Leave it empty until real sessions exist. Do not infer causal efficacy, accuracy or population-level findings from this small convenience sample.

## Data handling
Unfinished recordings can be withdrawn/deleted. Completed recordings can be exported or deleted through the session dialog. Files already shared must be deleted separately by their recipient on request. Usability records are separate from wardrobe backups. Only anonymous codes, consent timestamps, condition, completion events and answers are exported; free-text comments can still contain personal information, so review them before sharing. Agree a short retention period with participants (suggested: delete raw files within 30 days after analysis). Do not publish raw session files.
